Questions

Is this the government?

No. We're an independent service that reads the public SAM.gov listings and sends you the ones that match. You bid directly with the agency, and we never take a cut.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage or cancel" link at the bottom of any email or this page. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing.

Can I get more than one trade?

Yes. Each trade is its own $29/month subscription.

Do I need to be registered in SAM.gov?

To win a federal contract, yes (it's free at SAM.gov). You can start getting alerts first and register while you look at what's out there.
