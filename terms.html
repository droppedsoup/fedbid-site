Terms

Federal Bid Alerts ("we") emails you federal contract opportunities published on SAM.gov that match the trade you subscribe to. Each trade costs $5 per month on the Alerts plan or $25 per month on the Pro plan, after a 7-day free trial, billed by Stripe. Features marked "rolling out" are added to Pro at no extra cost as they become available.

Cancel anytime using the "Manage or cancel" link in any email. Cancelling during the trial means you are never charged. Cancelling later stops the next renewal; you keep alerts until the end of the paid month. If something went wrong, email us within 14 days of a charge and we'll refund it.

We copy listings from a public government source and can't promise they're complete or accurate. Always read the full notice on SAM.gov before bidding. Guides on this site are general information, not legal advice. We're not affiliated with SAM.gov or the U.S. government, and we're not liable for missed or lost bids.

Privacy

We keep your email address, your trade, plan and state choices, and which bids we've sent you. Stripe handles your card; we never see the full number. We don't sell or share your information, and we use it only to send your alerts and answer you. The bid search remembers your last filters in your own browser only. Email us to have your data deleted.
